# Installation Standards (Sample)

This is a sample document for demonstration. Replace it with your company's actual standards. The manufacturer's installation instructions and local building code always take priority over this document.

# Underlayment and ice barrier

Synthetic underlayment covers the full roof deck with 4-inch horizontal laps and 6-inch end laps. Ice and water shield is installed at all eaves, extending at least 24 inches inside the exterior wall line, and centered in all valleys at 36 inches wide. On low-slope sections between 2:12 and 4:12, the entire area gets ice and water shield.

# Shingle installation

Starter strip is installed along eaves and rakes. Shingles use 6 nails per shingle on all jobs, which qualifies for the manufacturer's high-wind rating. Nails must hit the manufacturer's nailing zone; overdriven or high nails are replaced before the crew leaves.

# Flashing

Drip edge goes on every eave and rake: under the underlayment at eaves, over it at rakes. Walls get new step flashing on every job; we do not reuse old step flashing. Valleys are closed-cut unless the customer chooses open metal valleys.

# Ventilation

We follow the 1/300 rule: 1 square foot of net free ventilation for every 300 square feet of attic floor, split evenly between intake at the soffits and exhaust at the ridge. If the attic lacks intake, we note it on the quote because ridge vent alone will not ventilate the attic properly.

# Steep and complex roofs

Roofs steeper than 8:12 require roof jacks and harness anchors on every job. Roofs steeper than 10:12 or with more than 12 facets are priced as complex and typically take one extra crew day.
