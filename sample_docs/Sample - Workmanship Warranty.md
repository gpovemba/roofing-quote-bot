# Workmanship Warranty (Sample)

This is a sample document for demonstration. Replace it with your company's actual warranty.

# What this warranty covers

We warrant our installation workmanship for 10 years from the date of completion on full roof replacements and new installs. Repairs carry a 2-year workmanship warranty on the repaired area only.

Workmanship covers leaks and failures caused by how the roof was installed: improper nailing, flashing we installed, underlayment laps, and ridge and hip cap attachment. If a covered leak occurs, we repair it and any interior drywall damage it caused, at no charge.

# What is not covered

This warranty does not cover damage from storms with wind gusts above the shingle's rated wind speed, hail, falling trees, ice dams on roofs where the owner declined ice and water shield, work done by others after our installation, or existing decking, chimneys, or skylights we did not replace.

Material defects are covered by the manufacturer's warranty, not this workmanship warranty. We register eligible manufacturer warranties on your behalf within 30 days of completion.

# Transfer

The workmanship warranty transfers once to a new owner if the home is sold. The new owner must notify us in writing within 60 days of the sale. There is no transfer fee.

# How to make a claim

Call or email us with photos of the leak or damage. We inspect within 5 business days, or within 48 hours for active leaks. Keep your final invoice; it is your proof of coverage.
