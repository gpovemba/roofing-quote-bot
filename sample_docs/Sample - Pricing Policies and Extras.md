# Pricing Policies and Extras (Sample)

This is a sample document for demonstration. Replace it with your company's actual policies.

# What every replacement quote includes

Every full replacement includes: removal of the layers stated on the quote, a dumpster and haul-away, synthetic underlayment over the full deck, ice and water shield at eaves and in valleys, new drip edge, starter strip, new pipe boots, ridge vent where the roof allows, the building permit, and a magnetic nail sweep of the yard and driveway at the end of each day.

# Decking replacement

Rotted or soft roof decking can only be found once the old roof is off. Our quotes include replacement of up to 2 sheets of decking at no extra charge. Additional sheets are $95 per 4x8 sheet of 7/16" OSB, installed. We photograph all damaged decking and show it to the homeowner before replacing it. On homes over 30 years old, homeowners should budget for 5 to 10 extra sheets.

# Common extras

Skylight re-flashing: $275 per skylight. Skylight replacement is quoted separately.
Chimney re-flashing with new step and counter flashing: $450 per chimney.
Gutter removal and reinstall: $4 per linear foot. New gutters are quoted separately.
Satellite dish removal and re-mount: $85.
Additional layer of tear-off found after the job starts: charged at our standard tear-off rate per square.

# Deposits and payment

We require a 30% deposit to order materials and schedule the job. The balance is due on completion after the homeowner walkthrough. We accept check, ACH, and credit card; card payments over $5,000 carry a 3% processing fee.

# Quote validity and scheduling

Quotes are valid for 30 days because supplier prices change. Most replacements are scheduled 2 to 4 weeks after the deposit. We do not tear off roofs when rain is forecast above 40% for the work day; weather delays do not change the quoted price.
